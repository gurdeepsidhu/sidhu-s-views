Our Story & Philosophy

Why I Started Sidhu's Views

In an online world dominated by endless 15-second video reels, hurried lifestyle hacks, and pressure to buy expensive showroom decor, Sidhu's Views was created as an antidote: a slower, calmer online magazine where ideas are given room to breathe.

I write about real life in Indian homes—where a balcony might only be four feet wide, where family budgets matter, and where a single cotton skein transformed into handmade coasters brings more genuine satisfaction than a cart full of factory plastic.

Transparency & Trust

Our Editorial Promise

  • Real Budgets, Real Tests: Every cost estimate (e.g., room makeover under ₹5,000 or balcony chai corner under ₹1,500) reflects genuine Indian market prices, local nursery visits, and itemized receipts.
  • No Hidden Sponsorships: Recommendations are genuinely based on personal experience and honest use, never sponsored brand placements.
  • Accessible Crafting: All crochet and craft patterns use clear US terminology, stitch count checklists, and step photos designed specifically for complete beginners.
