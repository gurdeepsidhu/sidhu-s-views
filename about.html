Personal Introduction

Hello, I'm Gurdeep.

I believe a beautiful life is not about having more, but about noticing more — the little details, the things we create with our hands, the spaces we make our own, and the style that feels truly ours.

Sidhu's Views is my personal space for the things I love, explore and think about — from fashion and beauty to home, creativity, crafts, travel and the quiet, everyday moments that make life meaningful.

My World

Fashion & Personal Style

Fashion, for me, is a world of possibilities rather than a single personal style. Through my work, I enjoy exploring everything from dressing and styling to hair, body-type guidance, trends and individual expressions of fashion — always with the reader's needs and curiosity in mind. Personally, I have always been drawn to the quiet elegance of timeless style.

Home & Décor

I see home décor as an invitation to explore — different styles, colours, materials and ways of creating a space that feels beautiful and personal. While I enjoy discovering many aesthetics for my readers, my own eye naturally leans towards elegant, minimal spaces, soft pastel colours and the warmth of handmade details.

Crafts & Creativity

Making things with my hands has always been one of the most enjoyable parts of my creative world. I work with crochet, embroidery, painting and fabric painting, and love turning simple materials into pieces for the home — from decorative embroidery and cushion covers to lanterns, fisherman lamps and other handmade creations.

The Journey

From Teaching to Writing, and from Writing to Creating

My professional journey began in education, as an English lecturer, a chapter that became fifteen years of teaching, learning and growing alongside my students.

But writing had been a part of me long before it became a profession. As a student, I enjoyed writing poetry and stories, never imagining that one day words would become part of my working life.

After leaving teaching, I found an opportunity to write professionally. What began as an opportunity to write articles for others gradually opened the door to many different worlds — fashion, lifestyle, informative content, promotional writing and subjects I might never have explored otherwise.

Over time, I discovered that writing is much more than putting words on a page. Before we write about something, we explore it, understand it and look at it from different perspectives. In that sense, writing has given me a way to experience many lives within one.

And perhaps that is what I love most about writing: it never lets me get bored. Every new subject brings a new question, a new discovery and another way of looking at life.

My creative side has much older roots. My mother was deeply creative and exceptionally skilled at embroidery, painting and crafts. I picked up those first little lessons from her when I was very young. Much later, when life gave me more time to explore my own interests, those early influences found their way back into my life.

I began experimenting, learning and creating more — from crochet and embroidery to painting, fabric art and home décor. Creating beautiful things with my hands became another way of exploring ideas and expressing myself.

Perhaps that is why a love for home and décor has never felt like just a hobby to me. It has always felt deeply woven into who I am.

And that, in many ways, is the journey behind Sidhu's Views — a life shaped by curiosity, creativity and the simple joy of discovering something new.

Things I Love

Books have always been a way of travelling without leaving a room. I enjoy reading across genres, following a story wherever it takes me, or simply discovering an idea that makes me look at something differently. Some books stay with me longer than others — The Magic of Thinking Big, The Alchemist, and Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay's Srikanta are among the ones I return to in thought.

I have the same curiosity about places. I love to travel, and while historical places have their own stories to tell, it is nature that draws me most. Give me mountains, open skies and a little distance from the noise of a city, and I am happy. Manali remains one of my favourite places.

And then there are the quiet moments.

There is something wonderfully simple about sitting completely still with a good book, with nowhere else to be and nothing demanding attention. That, perhaps, is one of the moments when I feel most like myself.

I have always believed that there is something beautiful waiting to be discovered in almost everything — an idea in a book, a colour in a room, a story behind a place, or simply the pleasure of making something with our own hands.

Sidhu's Views is my little corner of that curiosity. A place to explore, to create, to share ideas and to pause for a moment in a world that often moves too quickly.

I don't believe there is one perfect way to live beautifully. Perhaps the joy lies in discovering what feels beautiful and meaningful to each of us.

So, take what speaks to you, leave what doesn't, and perhaps discover something here that makes your own world a little more beautiful.

— Gurdeep
