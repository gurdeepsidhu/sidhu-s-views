How to Crochet an Easy Square Coaster: Beginner Step-by-Step Guide

A clear, professional tutorial for making a 4x4 inch cotton square coaster—with exact stitch counts, US terminology, matching step photos, and blocking tips.

By Gurdeep Sidhu • September 30, 2026 • 5 min read

Crocheting a simple square coaster is the single most practical beginner project you can make. It takes less than 45 minutes, uses about 15 grams of cotton yarn, and creates a durable, washable mug mat that protects wooden furniture from heat and water rings.

Below is the complete pattern written in standard US Crochet Terminology. Every step matches the demonstration photos below so you can follow along with 100% clarity!

Skill Level: Beginner / Easy
Finished Size: 4 inches x 4 inches (10 cm x 10 cm)
Yarn Needed: 100% Cotton Yarn (Worsted / Category 4), approx. 15 grams
Hook Size: 4.0 mm (US Size G/6)
Abbreviations: ch = chain • sc = single crochet • st(s) = stitch(es) • sl st = slip stitch
Step-by-Step Instructions
  1. Step 1: The Foundation Chain (Ch 16)
  2. Step 2: Working Row 1 (15 SC)
  3. Step 3: Crocheting Rows 2 to 16 (The Square Body)
  4. Step 4: Adding the Clean Edging Border
  5. Step 5: Wet Blocking for Crisp Flat Corners
  6. Frequently Asked Questions
Step 1 • Base Chain

Step 1: The Foundation Chain

Make a slip knot and place it on your crochet hook. Chain 16 loosely. This foundation chain should measure approximately 4 inches across.

Step 2 • Row 1

Step 2: Working Row 1

Insert hook into the 2nd chain from your hook and work 1 single crochet (sc). Work 1 sc in each remaining chain across to the end. You will have 15 single crochet stitches total. Chain 1, turn your work.

Step 3 • Rows 2 to 16

Step 3: Crocheting the Square Body (Rows 2–16)

Work 1 sc in the first stitch and in each stitch across the row. (15 sts). Chain 1, turn. Repeat this row until you have completed 16 rows total, forming a neat 4" x 4" square. Do not cut yarn yet!

Step 4 • Edging Border

Step 4: Adding the Clean Edging Border

To give your coaster crisp, professional edges:

  1. Single crochet evenly across the top row to the first corner. In the corner stitch, work (1 sc, ch 1, 1 sc) to turn smoothly.
  2. Single crochet into the end of each row down the side. In the next corner, work (1 sc, ch 1, 1 sc).
  3. Single crochet across the bottom loops. In the next corner, work (1 sc, ch 1, 1 sc).
  4. Single crochet up the remaining side. Work (1 sc, ch 1, 1 sc) in the final corner.
  5. Join with a slip stitch to your first border stitch. Cut yarn leaving a 4-inch tail, pull through, and weave in all loose ends with your yarn needle.

"Placing (SC, Ch 1, SC) in each corner prevents your square coaster from curling at the edges."

Step 5: Wet Blocking for Crisp Flat Corners

Freshly crocheted squares can sometimes look slightly wavy. Wet blocking sets the cotton fibers flat and gives your coaster a crisp, store-bought finish.

  1. Submerge your finished coaster in lukewarm water for 1–2 minutes.
  2. Gently press out excess water between a folded towel (do not wring or twist).
  3. Lay flat on a towel, pull the corners straight into a 4" x 4" square shape, and pin down with stainless steel T-pins.
  4. Let dry completely overnight—your coaster will stay 100% flat!

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is cotton yarn best for crocheting coasters?
100% cotton yarn is absorbent, heat-resistant, and machine washable. It holds condensation from cold drinks and protects wood tables from hot mugs without melting.
What size is a standard square crochet coaster?
A standard crochet coaster measures 4 inches by 4 inches (10 cm x 10 cm), which fits standard coffee mugs, teacups, and water glasses perfectly.
How do I prevent my square coaster edges from curling?
Working a single crochet border around all 4 sides with (SC, Ch 1, SC) in each corner keeps the edges neat, and wet blocking sets it completely flat.
