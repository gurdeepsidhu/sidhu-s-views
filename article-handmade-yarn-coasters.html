How to Crochet an Easy Round Coaster: Step-by-Step Beginner Guide

The simplest, most popular method to make a cozy cotton mug coaster in under 45 minutes—with clear stitch counts, easy steps, and helpful photos.

By Gurdeep Sidhu • September 30, 2026 • 5 min read

Crocheting a simple round coaster is the absolute best project for beginners. It takes less than 45 minutes, uses very little yarn, and gives you a beautiful handmade coaster to protect your wooden tables from hot chai cups!

This guide uses one clear, easy method—working in a round circle from the center outward. No complicated patterns or confusing steps—just 5 easy steps that anyone can follow!

Step-by-Step Overview
  1. Step 1: Get Your Materials Ready
  2. Step 2: Start the Center Loop (Round 1)
  3. Step 3: Expand the Circle (Round 2)
  4. Step 4: Finish the Outer Edge (Round 3)
  5. Step 5: Quick Wet Blocking for a Flat Finish
  6. Frequently Asked Questions

Step 1: Get Your Materials Ready

Before you begin, gather these 3 simple tools:

What You Need

• Yarn: 100% Cotton Yarn (DK or Worsted weight). Cotton is heat-resistant and absorbs water droplets!
• Crochet Hook: 4.0 mm (Size G-6) hook.
• Tools: Scissors and a yarn needle to tuck in loose ends.

Step 2: Start the Center Loop (Round 1)

We start from the center of the coaster and work in a circle.

Round 1 • Center Circle

How to do Round 1:

  1. Make a Magic Loop (or chain 4 and join with a slip stitch to form a small ring).
  2. Chain 3 (this counts as your first double crochet).
  3. Work 11 double crochets directly into the ring loop.
  4. Pull the yarn tail tight to close the center hole.
  5. Join with a slip stitch into the top of your starting chain-3.

Your stitch count at the end of Round 1: 12 stitches.

Step 3: Expand the Circle (Round 2)

Now we make the circle bigger so it can hold your mug.

Round 2 • Double Every Stitch

How to do Round 2:

  1. Chain 3. Work 1 double crochet in the exact same stitch where you joined.
  2. Work 2 double crochets into EVERY stitch all the way around.
  3. Join with a slip stitch to the top of your starting chain-3.

Your stitch count at the end of Round 2: 24 stitches.

Step 4: Finish the Outer Edge (Round 3)

This final round brings your coaster to full size (about 4.5 inches wide).

Round 3 • Final Border Round

How to do Round 3:

  1. Chain 3. Work 2 double crochets in the next stitch.
  2. Repeat this pattern around: [1 double crochet in next st, 2 double crochets in following st].
  3. Join with a slip stitch to the top of chain-3.
  4. Cut yarn leaving a 4-inch tail, pull through loop, and use your yarn needle to weave the tail securely into the back of your stitches.

Your stitch count at the end of Round 3: 36 stitches.

"By increasing stitches evenly in each round, your coaster stays flat and smooth under your favorite hot chai mug."

Step 5: Quick Wet Blocking for a Flat Finish

If your coaster curls up slightly when finished, don't worry! Here is the easy trick to make it lie 100% flat:

  1. Dip your coaster in water for 1 minute.
  2. Gently press out water with a clean towel (don't wring it).
  3. Lay it flat on a dry towel, press it flat into a circle, and let it dry.
  4. Once dry, your coaster will stay crisp, flat, and beautiful!

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is cotton yarn recommended for coasters?
Cotton yarn absorbs moisture from cold glasses and protects wooden surfaces from hot cups without scorching or losing shape.
How long does it take to complete one coaster?
It takes about 30 to 45 minutes for a beginner to finish one round coaster from start to finish.
How do I stop my round coaster from curling up?
Follow the exact stitch increases in each round (Round 1: 12 sts, Round 2: 24 sts, Round 3: 36 sts). Once done, dampen the coaster and let it dry flat on a towel.
