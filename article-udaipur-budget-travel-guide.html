Udaipur on a Budget: The Ultimate 3-Day Travel Guide to Lakes, Palaces & Hidden Alleys

By Sidhu's Views • Published on November 12, 2026 • 9 min read

Language / भाषा: 🇬🇧 English Edition | 🇮🇳 हिंदी में पढ़ें →

"Udaipur is often portrayed as an exclusive playground of five-star island palaces and celebrity weddings. But peel away the luxury brochures, and you discover an intimate city where ancient stone ghats, rooftop chai corners, and winding cobbled alleys cost almost nothing to enjoy."

Mention Udaipur to most travelers, and the first images that come to mind are opulent marble palaces floating in Lake Pichola with ₹40,000-a-night price tags. While royal grandeur is real, the true soul of the City of Lakes lives in its quiet public ghats, aromatic kachori stalls, and centuries-old havelis tucked behind historic city gates.

Whether you are visiting for the first time or returning for a peaceful winter escape, this friendly guide walks you through how to experience Udaipur deeply, slowly, and comfortably on a realistic budget of under ₹5,500 for three full days.

1. When to Visit & How to Reach Udaipur

Best Season: October through March is the golden window. During November and December, daytime temperatures hover pleasantly around 24°C with sunny skies, while evenings carry a refreshing chill perfect for sipping masala chai on a terrace. Summers (April to June) are scorching and best avoided.

By Train (Most Economical)

Direct overnight trains run daily from Delhi (Chetak / Mewar Express), Mumbai, and Jaipur. Udaipur Railway Station is just 3 km from the Old City.

By Flight

Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) is 22 km away. Pre-booked cabs or shared taxis run to the center for ₹600–₹800.

By Road

Overnight sleeper buses connect Udaipur seamlessly with Ahmedabad (4.5 hrs) and Jaipur (6 hrs).

2. Where to Stay: Heritage Havelis Under ₹2,000

Here is the biggest insider secret of Udaipur: You do not need to stay in a palace to wake up to palace views.

The old city surrounding Lake Pichola is dotted with family-run, 150-year-old traditional havelis converted into clean, welcoming guesthouses. Look around Hanuman Ghat, Chandpole, and Lal Ghat:

  • Hanuman Ghat (Facing City Palace): The quietest side. Guesthouses here face the City Palace across the water, offering million-dollar sunset reflections from rooftop cafes for ₹1,400–₹1,800/night.
  • Lal Ghat & Chandpole: Right in the heart of artisan alleys and cafes. Expect clean private rooms with traditional Rajasthani jharokhas for ₹1,200–₹1,900/night.
Budget Tip: Book a room with a basic fan/cooler in winter. The thick limestone haveli walls naturally keep rooms warm and cozy, saving you ₹500–₹700 per night on AC charges!

3. What to See: Iconic Monuments & Free Hidden Ghats

City Palace Complex

The crown jewel of Mewar architecture. Built over 400 years atop a ridge overlooking Lake Pichola, its peacock mosaics (Mor Chowk) and mirror-inlaid halls are stunning.

Budget Strategy: Regular entry tickets cost ₹300. Enter right when gates open at 9:00 AM. You will explore courtyards peacefully before tour buses arrive at 11:30 AM.

The Free Magic of Udaipur's Ghats

While paid monuments are grand, the real charm of Udaipur costs nothing:

  • Ambrai Ghat (Manjhi Ghat): Situated directly opposite City Palace. Arrive by 5:15 PM, sit on marble steps, and watch temple bells chime as palace lights ignite over shimmering water.
  • Gangaur Ghat: Right beside Bagore Ki Haveli. Perfect for morning walks, watching locals feed pigeons, and quiet journaling by the water.

Jagdish Temple & Saheliyon Ki Bari

Jagdish Temple: A 1651 AD architectural wonder of carved stone rising above old bazaar streets. Free entry. The 7:00 AM morning aarti with traditional cymbals is deeply calming.

Saheliyon Ki Bari: A lush royal garden built for court ladies, featuring lotus pools and gravity-fed fountains running without electricity. Entry is just ₹20.

4. What to Do: Cultural Experiences You Cannot Miss

Dharohar Folk Dance at Bagore Ki Haveli

If you do only one ticketed cultural show in Udaipur, make it this one. Every evening at 7:00 PM in the 18th-century courtyard of Bagore Ki Haveli, artists perform the Dharohar Dance Show.

For ₹100, you experience 60 minutes of vibrant puppetry, the spinning Chari dance with flaming pots, and the incredible Bhavai dance where an artist balances 9 brass pots on her head over glass! Arrive by 6:00 PM to get front-row cushion seats.

Lake Pichola Sunset Boat Ride (The Smart Way)

Cruises booked inside City Palace cost ₹800–₹1,000. Instead, head to the municipal jetty near Doodh Talai or Lal Ghat. Here, standard 45-minute shared boat rides cost just ₹250 to ₹350, providing the exact same glorious views of Lake Palace and Jag Mandir.

6. Where to Eat: Authentic Mewari Flavors on a Budget

You can feast like royalty in Udaipur for under ₹500–₹600 a day:

  • Jagdish Chowk Kachoris: Piping-hot onion and dal kachoris with tangy tamarind chutney (₹25–₹30) paired with kulhad chai (₹15) at Jagdish Mishthan Bhandar.
  • Krishna Dal Bati: Near Jal Mandir. For ₹250, enjoy an unlimited Rajasthani thali: ghee-soaked baatis, panchmel dal, sweet churma, gatte ki sabzi, and buttermilk.
  • Sukhadia Circle Street Food: Evening hotspot for pav bhaji, dahi chaat, and thick mawa kulfi (₹50–₹100).
  • Rooftop Ghat Cafes: Head to Hanuman Ghat rooftop cafes for comforting dal-roti or pizza while watching the illuminated palace reflection over the lake (₹200–₹350 per meal).

7. The Complete 3-Day Day-wise Itinerary & Budget

Day 1: City Palace, Ghat Sunsets & Dharohar Dance

  • Morning (9:00 AM): Explore City Palace early before crowds arrive.
  • Afternoon: Traditional thali lunch; stroll Chandpole's quiet stone alleys.
  • Evening (5:15 PM): Watch the sunset at Ambrai Ghat as palace lights turn on.
  • Night: Watch the Dharohar Dance Show at Bagore Ki Haveli; rooftop dinner.

Day 2: Royal Fountains, Lake Cruises & Bazaar Treasures

  • Morning: Kulhad chai and kachori at Jagdish Chowk; peaceful walk in Saheliyon Ki Bari.
  • Afternoon: Hearty Dal Bati Churma feast; handicraft shopping at Hathipole.
  • Evening (5:30 PM): Budget sunset boat ride on Lake Pichola from Doodh Talai jetty.
  • Night: Street food tastings at Sukhadia Circle.

Day 3: Nature Hike, Monsoon Palace & Souvenirs

  • Morning (7:30 AM): Scenic sunrise auto trip to Bahubali Hills overlooking Badi Lake.
  • Afternoon: Lakeside cafe brunch; pick up leather diaries and silver jewellery near Clock Tower.
  • Evening (5:00 PM): Sunset panorama over the city from Monsoon Palace (Sajjangarh).
  • Night: Relaxed lakeside dinner and farewell tea by the water.
