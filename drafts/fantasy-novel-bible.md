# तिलस्मी कोहरा: तीन बहनों की दास्तान (The Celestial Daughters)
### Master World & Character Bible

**Author / Creator:** Gurdeep Sidhu (गुरदीप सिधू)  
**Status:** In Active Development (Novel & Audio Series)

---

## 🌟 1. The Core Premise & Lore
- **The World:** A secluded village surrounded by ancient mountains and a dark, forbidden forest.
- **The Citadel of Shadows (शापित किला):** High upon the craggy peak sits an ancient fortress. It is not an ordinary castle, but a cosmic prison designed to contain powerful demons for the safety of the mortal world.
- **The 3 Imprisoned Demon Princes:** Trapped inside the citadel for millennia, seeking pure, pious celestial blood to perform the sacred resurrection ritual that will revive their ancient father.
- **The Pure Bloodline:** Unbeknownst to them, the three impoverished orphan sisters living with their ailing aunt are descendants of ancient celestials/angels, carrying the rarest pure blood.
- **The Higher Angelic Realm:** A peaceful, luminous, magical dimension where the angels originate, destined to be the sisters' ultimate home.
- **The Deep Forest & The Forest Prince:** Beneath the high citadel lies an enchanted, perilous forest where a mysterious Forest Prince rules with ancient powers of his own.

---

## 👥 2. The Characters

### The Three Sisters
1. **Nora (नोरा - बड़ी बहन):** 
   - Responsible, protective, selfless. 
   - Signs the recruitment contract to save her starving village and ailing aunt.
   - Imprisoned inside the citadel in a chamber with an illusion window showing an artificial garden. Held by the eldest Demon Prince.
2. **Bella (बेला - मँझली बहन):** 
   - Spirited, brave, deeply emotional.
   - When Nora vanishes, Bella undertakes the long journey to the citadel in a bullock cart accompanied by the handsome cart driver.
   - Almost plunges into the pit of bloodthirsty low-level demons, but is caught in a magical net and taken to the floating island!
3. **Ila (इला - सबसे छोटी बहन):** 
   - Innocent, gentle, perceptive.
   - Carries the dormant celestial power that will ultimately awaken to protect her family.
   - Guarded from afar by the second angel.

### The Disguised Angels (Fireflies / जुगनू)
1. **Ryan (रयान):** 
   - A celestial warrior sent to watch the sisters as a glowing firefly.
   - Disguises himself as a handsome young cart driver to accompany Bella on her journey.
   - Falls deeply, hopelessly in love with Bella; weeps outside the citadel gates knowing she cannot return.
2. **Zian (ज़ियान):** 
   - Ryan's celestial partner and friend.
   - Assigned to watch over little Ila.
   - Constantly reminds Ryan of celestial duty and warns him against mortal attachment.

### The Floating Island & The Guardians
1. **Grandma (दादी माँ / The Ancient Warden):**
   - The ancient, supreme caretaker of the citadel realm—far more powerful than the demon princes.
   - Bound by ancient cosmic oaths: cannot interfere in the demons' actions unless they break the universal laws.
   - After thousands of years of solitary vigil, she rescues Bella and loves her like her own granddaughter, using her immense creation magic to provide for her.
2. **Jojo (जोजो - The Talking Bird):**
   - Grandma's loyal, ancient, talking pet bird and self-proclaimed house manager.
   - Hilariously sassy, slightly jealous of Bella ("I've been Grandma's favorite for 3,000 years!").
   - Constantly squawks and scolds Bella whenever she wanders near the dangerous cliff edges of the floating island ("Hey! Step back, mortal! Do you want to become chutney in the forest below?!").
   - Deep down, fiercely loyal and protective of Bella.

### The Floating Island (जादुई तैरता द्वीप)
- A 100-meter landmass floating high in the sky above the deadly forest.
- Holds a cozy wooden house and a single, breathtaking **Golden Tree (सुनहरा पेड़)**.
- Walking off the cliff edges leads to a fatal plunge into the wild enchanted forest below.

## 📜 Chapter Progression Blueprint
- **Chapter 1:** The famine in the village, the glowing fireflies in the dark, the arrival of the gold-coin caravan from the dark citadel, and Nora's heartbreaking sacrifice.
- **Chapter 2:** The illusion room, Nora's captivity, and the realization in the village that Nora is lost.
- **Chapter 3:** Bella takes the bullock cart with Ryan (the disguised angel). The forbidden romance on the long road.
- **Chapter 4:** The gates of the citadel, the pit of ravenous demons, the sudden magical net, and the flight to the floating island.
- **Chapter 5:** Bella wakes up on the floating island with Grandma, the Golden Tree, and Jojo the sassy bird!
